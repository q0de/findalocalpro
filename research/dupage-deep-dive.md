## 4. Competitive Landscape

### Who Ranks for "[service] [town]" in DuPage

**Organic results dominated by:**
1. **Yelp** — Always top 3 for "[service] [city] IL"
2. **Angi (formerly Angie's List)** — Strong presence, directory pages
3. **HomeAdvisor** — Merged with Angi, still has separate pages ranking
4. **Thumbtack** — Growing but less dominant in suburbs
5. **Roto-Rooter** — Ranks for plumbing in most DuPage towns
6. **National chains** — Perfect Home Services, ABC Plumbing, OneStop Pro

**Local directories:** Very few. No DuPage-specific home services directory ranks well. This is our gap.

**Google Local Pack (Maps):** Dominated by actual local businesses with GMB profiles. This is where individual plumbers/HVAC companies show up.

**The opportunity:** Nobody is doing a hyper-local, DuPage-focused directory with programmatic SEO pages for every service+town combo. Yelp/Angi are national — we can out-local them.

- **Hyper-local focus:** "Verified Home Pros in Downers Grove" beats generic national directories

## 6. Seasonal Demand Calendar (Chicago Suburbs)

| Month | Hot Categories | Why |
|-------|---------------|-----|
| **Jan** | Furnace repair, pipe freezing, snow removal | Deep freeze, polar vortex risk |
| **Feb** | Furnace, plumbing (frozen pipes), insulation | Coldest month, pipes burst |
| **Mar** | HVAC tune-up, sump pump, gutter cleaning | Spring thaw, flooding risk |
| **Apr** | AC tune-up, landscaping, roofing inspection | Prep for summer, storm damage |
| **May** | AC install, deck/patio, pest control | Summer prep, termite season |
| **Jun** | AC repair, plumbing, electrical | Heat starts, usage spikes |
| **Jul** | AC emergency, plumbing, electrical | **PEAK AC month**. Heat waves. |
| **Aug** | AC repair, roofing, exterior painting | Storm season, continued heat |
| **Sep** | Furnace tune-up, gutter cleaning, roofing | Winter prep begins |
| **Oct** | Furnace install, weatherization, chimney | **PEAK HVAC overall**. First cold snaps. |
| **Nov** | Furnace repair, plumbing winterization | Cold setting in |
| **Dec** | Emergency furnace, pipe insulation, electrical | Holiday season, emergency calls |

**Key insight for Chicago suburbs:** We get BOTH extremes — brutal winters AND hot humid summers. That means HVAC is essentially year-round demand (heating Oct-Mar, cooling May-Sep). Plumbing peaks in winter (frozen pipes) and summer (heavy usage). Only ~6 weeks of "slow" season (April, late September).

**Revenue impact:** Unlike Sun Belt markets that only have AC season, Chicago gives us two peak seasons per year. This doubles the opportunity window.

## 7. Nextdoor Opportunity

### The Data
- **96% of Nextdoor users** have seen business recommendations
- **72% say** they've been influenced by a neighbor recommendation
- **71% have shared** a recommendation themselves
- **Home & Garden** is the #1 category for recommendations shared
- Suburban communities (like all of DuPage) are the most active

### Dave Mitchell Strategy Assessment
- DuPage County has ~15+ distinct Nextdoor neighborhoods covering our target towns
- Average suburban Nextdoor neighborhood sees **3-8 service recommendation requests per week**
- In a county this size, that's potentially **50-120 service requests per week** across all neighborhoods
- Dave Mitchell (our GC/remodeler persona) only needs to capture a fraction

### Conservative Nextdoor Projections
- Visible service requests Dave can respond to: 20-40/week
- Response rate (Dave drops the number): 50-70%
- Call-through rate (person actually calls): 15-25%
- **Estimated Nextdoor-sourced calls: 3-10/week → 12-40/month**

This is BEFORE any SEO traffic. Nextdoor alone could generate $600-2,800/month at $50/avg call.

## 9. Expansion Path (Post-DuPage)

### Priority Order
1. **Cook County Western Suburbs** — La Grange, Berwyn, Riverside, Brookfield, Oak Park. Dense, older homes, HUGE population. Borders DuPage directly. Many people already search "near Downers Grove" from these towns.

2. **Will County Northern Suburbs** — Bolingbrook, Romeoville, Plainfield, Joliet. Fast-growing, newer construction but increasingly needing service. Bolingbrook (75K pop) alone is a goldmine.

3. **Kane County Eastern Suburbs** — Aurora (180K!), Batavia, Geneva, St. Charles. Aurora is the second-largest city in Illinois. Massive opportunity.

4. **Kendall County** — Yorkville, Oswego. Exurban growth, newer homes but exploding population.

**Total addressable market across collar counties: ~3.5 million people, ~1.2 million households**

At full maturity across all collar counties: **$20,000-30,000/month** is very achievable.
