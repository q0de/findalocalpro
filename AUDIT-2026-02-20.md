## 🔴 CRITICAL (Fix Now — Blocking Rankings)

### 1. Missing Location Pages (SEO Signals: siteAuthority, geo)
**Impact: CRITICAL**

Per the local-service SEO template, the #1 architecture gap. FindALocalPro serves 12+ towns but has ZERO location pages. Google needs `/locations/downers-grove`, `/locations/naperville`, etc. to rank for "plumber in Naperville" or "electrician Downers Grove."

**Fix:**
```
/locations/
  /downers-grove
  /westmont
  /lisle
  /woodridge
  /darien
  /naperville
  /lombard
  /glen-ellyn
  /wheaton
  /hinsdale
  /oak-brook
  /bolingbrook
```

Each page: 500+ words unique content, local landmarks, service-specific testimonials, LocalBusiness schema with geo coordinates.

**Bonus:** Create `/locations/downers-grove/plumbing`, `/locations/naperville/hvac` combo pages for long-tail (e.g., "HVAC repair Naperville IL").

### 2. Missing /about, /contact, /reviews Pages
**Impact: CRITICAL**

404 on all three. Google's E-E-A-T framework requires:
- **/about** — Who runs this? Business story, team, credentials. Builds `authorObfuscatedGaiaStr` entity signals.
- **/contact** — NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency is a top-5 local ranking factor. Phone in header isn't enough.
- **/reviews** — Social proof page. AggregateRating schema. Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor.

### 3. No Google Business Profile Integration
**Impact: CRITICAL**

Per Whitespark 2026: business hours are a top-5 ranking factor. No GBP = invisible in local pack (the 3-pack map results that get 42% of clicks).

**Action:** Create GBP listing, sync NAP, add photos, get reviews.

### 4. Homepage H1 is Missing from HTML
**Impact: HIGH**

The H1 exists in JSX but isn't rendering as a proper `<h1>` tag in the HTML output — grep returns empty. The H1 content ("We Checked Their Paperwork. You Pick Your Pro.") is a great headline but needs to be a real `<h1>` tag for crawlers.

### 5. Sitemap Missing /services/roofing
**Impact: HIGH**

Roofing page exists (returns 200) and has 2 verified pros, but it's not in the sitemap. Also missing: `/get-matched` page.

## 🟡 HIGH PRIORITY (Fix This Week)

### 6. Schema Markup Gaps (SEO Signals: schema)
**Impact: HIGH**

**Homepage schema issues:**
- ✅ Organization, WebSite, ProfessionalService, FAQPage — good
- ❌ Missing `LocalBusiness` schema on homepage (critical for local SEO)
- ❌ ProfessionalService missing `telephone`, `address`, `geo`, `areaServed` with proper GeoCircle/GeoShape
- ❌ Missing `AggregateRating` (even if just trust score aggregate)

**Service pages:**
- ❌ No Service-specific schema (should have `@type: Service` with `provider` linking to LocalBusiness)
- ❌ No FAQ schema on service pages (they have FAQ-like content in the services list)

**Pro profile pages:**
- ✅ Have LocalBusiness schema — good
- ❌ Missing `AggregateRating`, `Review` schema
- ❌ `telephone: null` on some profiles — fix data

### 7. Title Tag Not Click-Optimized (Click-Driver + NavBoost)
**Impact: HIGH**

Current: `FindALocalPro — Find Trusted Home Service Pros Near You`

**Problems (per Click-Driver):**
- No geographic qualifier (kills local intent matching)
- No specificity (no numbers)
- No emotional trigger
- "Find Trusted" is generic — every competitor says this

**Rewrite options:**
- `Verified Home Pros in Downers Grove, IL | 4 Databases Checked | FindALocalPro`
- `FindALocalPro | 11 Verified Contractors in DuPage County | Free Matching`
- `Licensed Plumbers, Electricians & HVAC in Downers Grove | FindALocalPro`

Best: Include geo + number + differentiator. Title is the #1 NavBoost signal (titlematchScore).

### 8. Meta Description Not Compelling (Click-Driver)
**Impact: HIGH**

Current: "FindALocalPro — Find trusted, licensed home service professionals in your area..."

**Problems:**
- No geo specificity
- "in your area" is filler
- No urgency or differentiation
- Doesn't mention the 4-database verification (the USP!)

**Rewrite:**
`Every pro verified against 4 public databases — IDFPR license, BBB, SOS registration & BuildZoom. Free matching for Downers Grove & DuPage County homeowners. Call (630) 703-2607.`

### 9. No Internal Linking Strategy (SEO Signals: link equity)
**Impact: HIGH**

- Service pages don't link to each other
- No breadcrumbs
- No "Related Services" sections
- Pro profiles don't link to their service category page
- Homepage links TO services but services don't cross-link

**Fix:** Add breadcrumbs (Home > Services > Plumbing), related services sidebar, and "Other pros in this area" sections.

### 10. No Blog / Content Hub (Content Quality signals)
**Impact: HIGH**

Zero blog content = zero informational keyword coverage. Local service sites need:
- "How to choose a plumber in Downers Grove"
- "Signs you need furnace repair"
- "Do I need a permit for electrical work in DuPage County?"

This builds `OriginalContentScore`, `contentEffort`, and topical authority.

## 🟢 CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION (Click-Driver + Dopamine + Copy Critique)

### 11. Hero CTA Copy (Conversion Copy Critique)
**Impact: MEDIUM-HIGH**

Current CTA: "Get Matched Free"

**Problems:**
- Doesn't specify what happens next (reduces trust)
- "Free" is overused, triggers skepticism
- No urgency

**Rewrites:**
- `Chat with a Local Pro Now` (specificity + immediacy)
- `Find Your Verified Pro in 60 Seconds` (time specificity)
- `See Licensed Pros Near You` (benefit-first)

### 12. Social Proof Numbers Are Too Small (Dopamine + Copy)
**Impact: MEDIUM**

Current counters: "4 Databases Checked · 11+ Verified Pros · 12 Towns Served · 60s Average Match Time"

**Problems:**
- 11 pros is honest but unimpressive. Competitors claim thousands.
- "4 databases" is good but needs reframing

**Fix:** Reframe around impact:
- "4 Government Databases Cross-Referenced" (authority)
- "137 License Records Checked" (bigger number, same truth)
- "12 DuPage County Towns Covered" (add county)
- "Under 60 Seconds to Get Matched" (reframe as benefit)

### 13. Callback Form is Buried + Weak (Conversion Copy)
**Impact: MEDIUM**

The ServiceCallbackForm has:
- Generic "Request a Callback" heading (not benefit-driven)
- No trust signals near the form
- No urgency or social proof
- Submits to `alert()` — doesn't actually work!

**Fix:**
- Heading: "Get a Call from a Licensed [Trade] Pro" 
- Add trust badge strip above form
- Add "Average response: under 5 minutes"
- ACTUALLY WIRE IT to Supabase/Twilio (critical!)

### 14. No Sticky CTA on Mobile (UX/Dopamine)
**Impact: MEDIUM**

Mobile users lose the CTA as they scroll. Need a sticky bottom bar with phone + chat.

### 15. Missing Micro-Interactions (Dopamine Learning)
**Impact: MEDIUM**

The animations (AnimatedSection, CountUp, StaggeredGrid) are good starts. Missing:
- Hover feedback on pro cards (subtle lift + shadow — partially there)
- Click ripple effect on CTAs
- Form field focus animations
- Success state celebrations after form submission
- Loading states during data fetches
