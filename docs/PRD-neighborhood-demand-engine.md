# PRD: Neighborhood Demand Engine

## 1. Overview

**Product name:** Neighborhood Demand Engine  
**Project:** FindALocalPro Partner Growth Program  
**Primary customer:** Local home-service business owners, starting with plumbers, electricians, HVAC, appliance repair, pest control, handyman, roofing, and similar high-intent service categories.  
**Owner-facing promise:** We monitor the local internet for people who need your service, help turn those signals into booked jobs, and keep you aware of reputation risks before they cost you business.

FindALocalPro already has active local demand infrastructure through SEO pages, eLocal routing, dedicated call flows, and Nextdoor lead discovery. The Neighborhood Demand Engine expands the partner package from “featured placement and Nextdoor replies” into a broader, higher-value monthly service: local demand monitoring, reputation alerts, call tracking, weekly reporting, and lightweight competitive intelligence.

The goal is to make the partner offer feel hard to pass up because the business owner sees multiple channels working together on their behalf, not just one lead source.

---

## 2. Problem

Local home-service owners miss demand because it is scattered across many places:

- Homeowners ask for help on Nextdoor and local Facebook groups.
- Google reviews appear without timely owner responses.
- Google Business Profile questions or listing issues go unnoticed.
- Missed calls are not tracked by source.
- Competitors get repeatedly recommended in neighborhood conversations.
- Owners do not have a simple weekly view of what happened locally.

Most contractors do not have time to monitor all of this. Agencies sell SEO, ads, or review management separately, but small local operators often need a simple, practical “watch the neighborhood and tell me where the money is” service.

---

## 3. Goals

### Business goals

1. Increase perceived value of the FindALocalPro partner package.
2. Justify a recurring monthly price point beyond pay-per-lead economics.
3. Create a defensible local-growth service that combines lead generation, reputation monitoring, and market intelligence.
4. Generate partner retention through visible weekly output, not vague “SEO is working” promises.
5. Start with manual/semi-automated operations before building a full dashboard.

### Customer goals

1. See local homeowner demand they would otherwise miss.
2. Get fast alerts for hot opportunities and reputation issues.
3. Understand which towns, services, and channels are producing demand.
4. Improve review response quality and review volume.
5. Know which competitors are being recommended locally.
6. Get a simple report without logging into another tool.

---

## 4. Non-Goals

- Building a full client portal for v1.
- Fully autonomous posting on every social platform.
- Guaranteeing booked jobs or revenue.
- Replacing the owner’s CRM.
- Running paid ads.
- Managing full social media content calendars.
- Acting as the business owner’s personal social media account without review/approval where platform risk is high.

## 7. Core Features

### 7.1 Nextdoor Lead Monitoring

**Description:** Monitor relevant local Nextdoor conversations for homeowner service requests.

**Examples:**

- “Need a plumber in Downers Grove.”
- “Any recommendations for an electrician?”
- “Looking for someone to fix my AC.”
- “Who does appliance repair near Naperville?”

**Capabilities:**

- Keyword/category monitoring.
- Service-area matching.
- Duplicate detection.
- Human-looking response drafts or guarded posting workflow.
- Tracking IDs for posted replies where supported.
- Lead outcome logging when known.

**Important guardrails:**

- Avoid spam behavior.
- Use locally appropriate language.
- Keep replies text-only unless a platform-safe branded asset is intentionally approved.
- Avoid AI-looking punctuation and over-polished language.
- Respect account geography and profile separation.

---

### 7.2 Facebook Local Group Monitoring

**Description:** Monitor relevant local Facebook groups/pages for homeowner service intent where access and platform rules allow.

**Capabilities:**

- Identify public/local group posts mentioning target services.
- Flag hot opportunities for owner follow-up.
- Draft reply suggestions where direct posting is not appropriate.
- Track repeated recommendations and local competitor names.

**V1 approach:** Semi-manual or assisted monitoring. Treat Facebook as an add-on channel until operational reliability and compliance are proven.

---

### 7.3 Google Review Monitoring

**Description:** Watch the partner’s Google reviews and flag changes.

**Capabilities:**

- Detect new reviews.
- Flag negative or neutral reviews quickly.
- Draft owner response suggestions.
- Identify happy customers who may be worth follow-up or testimonial requests.
- Track rating/review-count trend over time.

**Value:** Reputation issues are time-sensitive. A bad unanswered review can cost more than the monthly package.

---

### 7.4 Google Business Profile Support

**Description:** Lightweight GBP hygiene and visibility support.

**Capabilities:**

- Monitor Q&A where possible.
- Review service categories and business description for obvious gaps.
- Suggest Google Posts or updates.
- Track GBP link/call activity if accessible.
- Recommend review-request cadence.

**V1 constraint:** Full GBP API integration may require owner authorization. Start with manual/assisted checks and expand after partner onboarding.

---

### 7.5 Dedicated Call Tracking

**Description:** Use a dedicated FindALocalPro partner tracking line where appropriate.

**Capabilities:**

- Route calls to the business.
- Log call source/category where known.
- Track answered vs missed calls.
- Surface hot-call alerts.
- Include call volume in weekly report.

**Existing FindALocalPro infrastructure:** Twilio call routing, service-demand logging, and partner lead routing already exist and can support this package.

---

### 7.6 Featured FindALocalPro Placement

**Description:** Partner receives enhanced visibility on relevant FindALocalPro pages.

**Capabilities:**

- Featured placement for selected service/town/category.
- Stronger call-to-action.
- Trust/reputation snippets where available.
- Partner tracking line or direct routing.

**Value:** Gives the package an owned-channel asset, not only third-party monitoring.

---

### 7.7 Competitor Watch

**Description:** Track which local competitors are repeatedly recommended or visible in homeowner conversations.

**Capabilities:**

- Count recurring competitor mentions.
- Identify top recommended providers by town/service.
- Capture common reasons competitors are recommended.
- Identify gaps in partner positioning.

**Example insight:**

> “This week, ABC Plumbing was recommended 4 times in Naperville threads, usually for emergency drain cleaning. We should emphasize same-day drain cleaning in your responses and GBP posts.”

---

### 7.8 Review Generation Assist

**Description:** Provide simple scripts/templates the owner can use to ask satisfied customers for reviews.

**Capabilities:**

- SMS review request template.
- Email review request template.
- Job-completion follow-up script.
- Weekly reminder: “Ask these customers for reviews.”

**Optional future enhancement:** Owner submits completed jobs, FindALocalPro generates review request drafts.

---

### 7.9 Weekly Owner Report

**Description:** A simple weekly email/PDF-style report. This is the v1 owner view instead of a dashboard.

**Name:** Local Opportunity Report or Neighborhood Demand Report.

**Recommended name:** **Neighborhood Demand Report**

**Sections:**

1. Executive snapshot
2. New local opportunities found
3. Actions taken
4. Lead/call outcomes
5. Reputation alerts
6. Competitor/local chatter
7. Recommended next moves

**Example summary:**

> This week we found 11 local service opportunities across Nextdoor, search/call activity, and review signals. We replied to 3 homeowner requests, routed 2 calls, flagged 1 review needing a response, and saw ABC Plumbing recommended repeatedly for emergency work in Naperville.

---

### 7.10 Real-Time Alerts

**Description:** Send urgent alerts outside the weekly report when timing matters.

**Alert triggers:**

- Hot lead found.
- Someone asks for a direct recommendation.
- New negative Google review.
- Missed call.
- Repeated competitor mention spike.
- Homeowner follow-up asks for contact info.

**Delivery options:**

- Email
- SMS
- Telegram/internal operator alert during v1

## 8. Owner View / Reporting UX

### V1: Email-first reporting

Do not build a dashboard for v1. Use weekly email reports plus urgent alerts.

**Rationale:**

- Lower build cost.
- No client login friction.
- Easier to sell as high-touch service.
- Allows learning before committing to dashboard design.
- Contractors are more likely to read a concise email than log into another portal.

### V1 report cadence

- Weekly report sent Monday morning.
- Real-time alerts as needed.
- Monthly strategy recap optional for higher tiers.

### V2: Lightweight dashboard

Only build after multiple paying partners prove report demand.

Potential dashboard modules:

- Opportunities timeline
- Calls/leads by source
- Review alerts
- Competitor mentions
- Service/town heatmap
- Report archive
- Partner settings

## 13. Operational Workflow

### Weekly workflow

1. Pull partner opportunities from Nextdoor/local monitoring logs.
2. Pull call-tracking activity.
3. Check Google reviews.
4. Check GBP/Q&A manually or through available integration.
5. Summarize competitor mentions.
6. Generate Neighborhood Demand Report.
7. Send to owner.
8. Log report in partner_reports.

### Real-time workflow

1. Detect hot lead or reputation alert.
2. Classify severity.
3. Send alert to internal operator or partner.
4. Track action taken.
5. Include in weekly report.

## 15. Risks and Mitigations

### Platform risk

**Risk:** Nextdoor/Facebook may limit automation or posting behavior.  
**Mitigation:** Keep v1 guarded, human-reviewed, locally appropriate, and avoid bulk/spammy patterns.

### Reporting labor risk

**Risk:** Weekly reports become too manual.  
**Mitigation:** Standardize data collection and report generation early. Automate summaries only after manual process is stable.

### Attribution disputes

**Risk:** Owner questions whether a job came from FindALocalPro.  
**Mitigation:** Use tracking numbers, source logs, timestamps, and conservative reporting language.

### Overpromising

**Risk:** Selling guaranteed jobs instead of opportunity monitoring.  
**Mitigation:** Position as local demand/reputation monitoring with lead routing, not guaranteed booked revenue.

### Compliance/reputation risk

**Risk:** Public replies look automated or inappropriate.  
**Mitigation:** Maintain response guardrails, local context checks, and no fully autonomous public posting in new geographies without review.
